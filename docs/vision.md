# Kyrion Vision

## What is Kyrion?

Kyrion is a local-first platform that connects personal devices, services,
media, home automation and artificial intelligence through one unified system.

Instead of requiring users to manage many isolated applications, Kyrion provides
a central interface through which they can observe, control and automate their
digital environment.

## Why does Kyrion exist?

Modern digital environments are fragmented.

Smart-home devices, computers, media servers, calendars, email accounts and
other services often require separate applications, cloud accounts and
proprietary ecosystems.

This creates several problems:

- Users lose control over where their data is processed.
- Devices from different manufacturers do not communicate well.
- Everyday actions require switching between many applications.
- Existing platforms are often technically powerful but difficult to configure.
- Artificial intelligence is commonly cloud-dependent and disconnected from
  the user's own infrastructure.

Kyrion aims to provide a coherent alternative.

## Product vision

Kyrion should become a personal infrastructure platform that:

- runs primarily on hardware controlled by the user;
- connects otherwise isolated systems;
- remains usable without artificial intelligence;
- uses AI as an optional and controlled interaction layer;
- offers a modern and intuitive user experience;
- allows integrations to be added through a modular architecture;
- works for individual users first and may later expand to households and
  organisations.

## Initial user

The first user of Kyrion is its creator.

Every initial feature must solve a real problem or provide meaningful daily
value. Features should not be implemented only because they appear impressive
or technically interesting.

## Long-term direction

In the long term, Kyrion may connect:

- smart-home devices;
- computers and network devices;
- local media services such as Jellyfin;
- calendars and email accounts;
- photovoltaic and energy systems;
- local files and documents;
- automations and scheduled workflows;
- local and optional cloud-based AI models;
- mobile, web and voice interfaces.

Kyrion is not intended to replace every specialised platform.

It should provide a coherent layer above them and make their capabilities
accessible through one consistent system.
